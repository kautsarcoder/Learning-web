1. Who we are

LearningSpace ("we", "us") is a web application for learning languages, including Levantine Arabic, available at https://learningspace-kyo.my.id. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use, store, share and protect it, and the choices you have. It applies to the website and to sign-in with email/password or with your Google Account.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Data you provide or that comes from sign-in

2.2 Data generated while you use the app

  • Learning progress: lessons, units and sections you completed, scores, streaks, and which exercises you answered correctly or incorrectly.
  • Vocabulary progress: mastery level and review schedule for each word, used to decide what to review next.
  • Preferences: interface language and similar settings.
  • Text you ask the app to look up or translate (for example a single Arabic word or sentence you tap for its meaning).

2.3 Technical data

  • Basic request logs generated by our hosting provider (IP address, browser type, requested page, time, error details) used for security, debugging and abuse prevention.
  • Browser storage (localStorage / sessionStorage) used to keep you signed in and to cache lessons, audio and progress so the app loads faster. This is functional storage, not advertising.

We do not collect your contacts, files, precise location, payment card details, or any sensitive personal data such as health, religion or political views.

3. Google user data (Sign in with Google)

If you choose to sign in with Google, we request only the basic scopes openid, email and profile. Through them we receive your email address, your name, your profile picture and your Google account identifier. We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google service, and we do not access your Google data after sign-in beyond what is listed here.

LearningSpace's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • We use this data only to create and operate your account and show your profile inside the app.
  • We do not sell Google user data, do not use it for advertising or profiling, and do not transfer it to third parties except the service providers listed in section 5 that are necessary to operate the app.
  • No human reads your Google user data except where needed for security, to comply with law, or with your explicit consent.

You can revoke LearningSpace's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4. How we use your data

  • To provide the service: authenticate you, save and restore your progress, and show your profile.
  • To personalise learning: choose which words and exercises to review based on your progress.
  • To operate and secure the service: prevent abuse, fix bugs and keep the app reliable.
  • To communicate with you: respond to messages you send and send essential account notices.
  • To meet legal obligations when required.

We do not sell personal data, we do not show third-party advertising, and we do not use your data to train third-party AI models.

6. Where data is stored and international transfers

Our providers operate servers in several countries, so your data may be processed outside the country where you live. We choose reputable providers that apply industry-standard safeguards.

7. Retention

  • Account and learning data are kept for as long as your account exists.
  • If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and associated learning data within 30 days, except where we must keep limited information to comply with law or resolve disputes.
  • Server logs are kept for a short period (typically up to 30 days) and then deleted or anonymised.

8. Security

We protect data with encrypted connections (HTTPS), access-controlled databases with row-level security, hashed passwords, and restricted server-side keys that are never exposed in the browser. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data and fix issues promptly.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: you can view your profile in the app and ask us for a copy or correction of your data.
  • Deletion: you can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by emailing the address in section 11 from the email linked to your account.
  • Revoke Google access: via your Google Account permissions page (link in section 3).
  • Browser storage: you can clear localStorage/sessionStorage in your browser; you will be signed out and local caches will be removed.
  • Objection / restriction: you may object to or ask us to restrict certain processing; contact us and we will respond.

Depending on where you live (for example under Indonesian data protection law, the GDPR or similar laws), you may have additional rights. We will honour them on request.

10. Children

LearningSpace is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it. Where local law sets a higher age for consent, users below that age should use the app with a parent or guardian's permission.
